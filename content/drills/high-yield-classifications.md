# 1. Beta-Adrenoceptor Antagonists (Beta Blockers)

```
                     BETA-ADRENOCEPTOR ANTAGONISTS
                                   │
      ┌────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────┐
      ▼                            ▼                            ▼
NON-SELECTIVE (β1 + β2)    CARDIOSELECTIVE (β1)        ALPHA + BETA BLOCKERS
  - Without ISA:              - Metoprolol               - Labetalol (IV in HTN crisis)
      * Propranolol           - Atenolol                 - Carvedilol (in Heart Failure)
      * Timolol (glaucoma)    - Bisoprolol
      * Sotalol (K+ blocker)  - Esmolol (ultra-short)
  - With ISA (Intrinsic):     - Nebivolol (releases NO)
      * Pindolol
```
