## 🎯 Today's Core Learning Objectives
- [ ] Antimalarials: Artemisinin-based Combination Therapy (ACT), Chloroquine mechanism (heme polymerase) and resistance, Primaquine (radical cure of P. vivax & G6PD hemolysis)
- [ ] Anticancer Principles: Cell cycle specific (CCS) vs non-specific (CCNS) agents
- [ ] Methotrexate: DHFR inhibition & Leucovorin rescue; Cyclophosphamide: Acrolein hemorrhagic cystitis & Mesna
- [ ] Anticoagulants: Unfractionated Heparin vs LMWH (Enoxaparin) vs Warfarin (PT/INR monitoring)
- [ ] Direct Oral Anticoagulants (DOACs): Dabigatran (thrombin inhibitor) vs Apixaban/Rivaroxaban (Factor Xa inhibitors)
- [ ] Fibrinolytics (Alteplase) and Antiplatelets (Clopidogrel, Aspirin)

---

## 💡 High-Yield Exam Pearls (Must-Memorize)
> ⚡ Primaquine: Only drug that kills hypnozoites (dormant liver stage) of P. vivax/ovale; MUST test for G6PD deficiency before administration to prevent acute intravascular hemolysis.
>
> ⚡ Heparin: Antidote is Protamine Sulfate; monitor with aPTT.
>
> ⚡ Warfarin: Antidote is Vitamin K1 + FFP; monitor with PT/INR.
>
> ⚡ Cyclophosphamide urotoxicity: Prevented by aggressive hydration + MESNA (Sodium 2-mercaptoethanesulfonate).
