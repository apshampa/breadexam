---

## 💡 High-Yield Exam Pearls (Must-Memorize)
> ⚡ Physostigmine is a tertiary amine (crosses blood-brain barrier); Neostigmine is quaternary (no BBB penetration, stronger nicotinic action at neuromuscular junction).
>
> ⚡ Tensilon (Edrophonium) test: Improves myasthenic crisis within 60 seconds; worsens cholinergic crisis.
>
> ⚡ Atropine toxicity: 'Blind as a bat, dry as a bone, red as a beet, hot as a hare, mad as a hatter'.
