## 💡 High-Yield Exam Pearls (Must-Memorize)
> ⚡ Enzyme Inducers: **G**riseofulvin, **P**henytoin, **R**ifampicin, **S**moking, **C**arbamazepine, **P**henobarbitone.
>
> ⚡ Enzyme Inhibitors: **V**alproate, **K**etoconazole, **C**iprofloxacin, **C**imetidine, **E**rythromycin, **I**soniazid.
>
> ⚡ Loading Dose = (Target plasma conc × Vd) / Bioavailability (F).
>
> ⚡ Maintenance Dose = (Target plasma conc × Clearance) / Bioavailability (F).
