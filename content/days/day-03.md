## 💡 High-Yield Exam Pearls (Must-Memorize)
> ⚡ Adrenaline is drug of choice for Anaphylaxis (1:1000 IM, 0.5 mL in adult).
>
> ⚡ In Pheochromocytoma: ALWAYS give Alpha-blocker (Phenoxybenzamine) BEFORE Beta-blocker.
>
> ⚡ Tamsulosin: Selective α1A blocker for BPH; minimal effect on blood pressure (no postural hypotension).
>
> ⚡ Beta-blockers with intrinsic sympathomimetic activity (ISA): Pindolol, Acebutolol (cause less bradycardia).
