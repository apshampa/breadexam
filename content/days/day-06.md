---

## 💡 High-Yield Exam Pearls (Must-Memorize)
> ⚡ Benzodiazepines: increase FREQUENCY of Cl- opening; Barbiturates: increase DURATION of Cl- opening (Benza-F-requent, Barbi-D-urate).
>
> ⚡ Drug of choice for Absence (Petit Mal) seizures: Ethosuximide (blocks T-type Ca2+ channels in thalamus) or Sodium Valproate.
>
> ⚡ Drug of choice for Trigeminal Neuralgia: Carbamazepine.
>
> ⚡ Levodopa is co-administered with Carbidopa (peripheral dopa decarboxylase inhibitor) to prevent peripheral conversion to dopamine, reducing nausea and arrhythmia.
