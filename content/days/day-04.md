## 💡 High-Yield Exam Pearls (Must-Memorize)
> ⚡ ACE Inhibitors cause dry cough and angioedema by preventing Bradykinin breakdown (Kininase II inhibition).
>
> ⚡ Verapamil is contraindicated with Beta-blockers (risk of severe bradycardia, AV block, and cardiac arrest).
>
> ⚡ Nitrate tolerance is overcome by providing a daily 8-12 hour nitrate-free interval.
>
> ⚡ In Pre-eclampsia / Gestational HTN: Labetalol, Methyldopa, Nifedipine, Hydralazine are safe; ACEi/ARBs are strictly contraindicated.
