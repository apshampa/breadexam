## 💡 High-Yield Exam Pearls (Must-Memorize)
> ⚡ Digoxin toxicity: Most common symptom is anorexia/nausea; most characteristic ocular symptom is xanthopsia (yellow-green vision); most common arrhythmia is ventricular bigeminy; most specific arrhythmia is Non-paroxysmal AV junctional tachycardia with AV block.
>
> ⚡ Loop diuretics act on Na+-K+-2Cl- symporter in Thick Ascending Limb of Henle; they cause hypocalcemia (Loops Lose Calcium).
>
> ⚡ Thiazides act on Na+-Cl- symporter in Distal Convoluted Tubule; they cause hypercalcemia (Thiazides Take Calcium).
>
> ⚡ Adenosine: Drug of choice for acute PSVT conversion (rapid IV push followed by saline flush).
