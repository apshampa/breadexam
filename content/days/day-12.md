## 💡 High-Yield Exam Pearls (Must-Memorize)
> ⚡ PTU is preferred in the 1st trimester of pregnancy because Methimazole is teratogenic (causes aplasia cutis and choanal atresia). Methimazole is preferred in 2nd/3rd trimesters due to PTU hepatotoxicity.
>
> ⚡ Lugol's Iodine inhibits thyroid hormone release immediately (Wolff-Chaikoff effect); used pre-operatively before thyroidectomy to reduce vascularity.
>
> ⚡ Combined OCPs: Inhibit ovulation by suppressing LH surge (progestin) and FSH (estrogen); contraindicated in women >35 who smoke (high risk of thromboembolism).
